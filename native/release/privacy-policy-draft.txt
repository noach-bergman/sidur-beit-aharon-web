Siddur Beit Aharon VeYisrael — Privacy Policy

The current iPhone app reads its bundled siddur pages and calculates calendar information and prayer times on the device. It does not require an account and does not include advertising or a developer-operated analytics service.

Optional location
If you grant location permission, the app obtains your location to calculate local prayer times. The selected coordinates and location settings are saved locally on the device. The app code does not send those coordinates to the publisher's server. You can decline location permission and select a city manually. You can revoke location access in iPhone Settings; choose a city in the app to replace the saved coordinates.

Saved preferences
The app saves your last reading page, selected location, highlighting preference, guide completion and guide language locally so they can be restored. These preferences are not uploaded to a publisher-operated service. Deleting the app removes its local app data; device backup behavior is governed by your Apple settings.
